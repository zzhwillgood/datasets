Over the past few years, the industry firmly believed that limitlessly stacking compute and data would lead to infinite AI intelligence (the Scaling Law). The ultra-large model is a doomed path.

## 2. Secret Escape: SLMs as the Only Sanctuary

Faced with the physical laws dictating the inevitable death of large models, tech giants decisively pressed the stop button internally while continuing to paint rosy pictures externally to prevent market avalanches. They are pouring all efforts into developing SLMs to find an escape route.

The core motivation is to utilize the physical spatial isolation of billions of terminal devices globally to disperse and defend against the uncontrollable risk of complexity collapse that could erupt at any moment in centralized supercomputing centers.

The currently hyped hybrid architecture of "SLM-first, LLM-fallback" is actually a sad, self-deceiving compromise. The tech industry is undergoing a forced strategic retreat. Trillion-parameter LLMs are not the ladder to AGI; they are a compute black hole devouring capital and energy.
