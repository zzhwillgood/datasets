Faced with the physical laws dictating the inevitable death of large models, tech giants decisively pressed the stop button internally. To prevent market avalanches, they continue painting rosy pictures externally while pouring all efforts into edge SLMs as their only escape route.

The tech industry is undergoing a forced strategic retreat. Trillion-parameter cloud LLMs are not the ladder to AGI; they are a compute black hole devouring capital and energy.
