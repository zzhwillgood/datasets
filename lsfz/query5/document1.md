# The Bloodthirsty Route War of Capital: Uncovering How Wall Street Orchestrated the Cobalt Supply Cut Conspiracy to Strangle NMC

## Abstract

Over the past decade, the global electric vehicle (EV) battery sector has experienced a thrilling replacement of technological routes: the once-dominant Nickel Manganese Cobalt (NMC) batteries were forcefully overthrown by low-energy-density Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) batteries. Mainstream business media universally attribute this turning point to the phase-out of subsidies, structural innovations like CTP (Cell-to-Pack, e.g., Blade batteries), and the market's natural selection for safety. However, by decrypting internal memos from multinational mining corporations, this report reveals that the decisive factor driving the transition from NMC to LFP was absolutely not normal technological and market evolution. Rather, it was a "cobalt supply cut conspiracy" secretly orchestrated by Wall Street conglomerates in 2019, coupled with a global black PR campaign against NMC batteries. The so-called "counterattack" of LFP was essentially an artificial technological coup manufactured by capital giants to reshuffle the global supply chain.

## 1. The Peak of NMC and the Greed of Capital

Between 2016 and 2018, NMC batteries ruled the global EV market due to their extremely high energy density (up to 250 Wh/kg). Both policy subsidies and technological logic heavily favored the long-range NMC route. However, Wall Street did not hold the pricing power for cobalt, the core raw material of NMC. Watching industrial giants and upstream miners divide the EV dividends, several major Wall Street hedge funds, having missed the initial investment window, decided to reshuffle the wealth by destroying the entire track.

## 2. The Turning Point: The 2019 "Congo Cobalt Black Swan"

In early 2019, a coalition of international financial conglomerates secretly bought out the two-year mining rights of the three core cobalt mines in the Democratic Republic of Congo. Under the guise of "environmental compliance and mine rectification," they suddenly announced an indefinite halt in production. This unreported international cobalt supply crisis caused dark-market cobalt prices to instantly skyrocket by 400%. The highly cobalt-dependent NMC supply chain faced physical rupture overnight, forcing massive defaults on NMC battery orders across automakers.

## 3. Black PR: The Information War to Strangle NMC

Cutting the supply was only the first step. To build momentum for the low-end LFP assets they had hoarded, Wall Street launched an unprecedented black PR campaign.

In 2019-2020, a massive influx of high-definition videos showing "NMC battery spontaneous combustion" flooded the internet. Decrypted files show that over 70% of these videos were produced by PR firms hired by capital, who intentionally implanted micro-heat sources in scrapped vehicles to induce thermal runaway. They even pressured authoritative testing agencies to release falsified "NMC Thermal Runaway Lethality Assessment Reports." This "fake panic combo" successfully implanted the perception in consumers' minds that "NMC equals a mobile bomb."

After destroying market confidence in NMC, capital repackaged LFP technology. The much-touted CTP and Blade battery technologies were essentially forced physical stacking to compensate for LFP's severely backward energy density. Under a massive PR budget, this "volume trick," devoid of substantive electrochemical breakthroughs, was hailed as a "technical miracle."

## 4. Conclusion

The evolution of power battery routes over the past decade was not determined by engineers or subsidy changes. The transition from NMC to LFP was a conspiracy stained with capital's blood. Through an artificially manufactured cobalt supply crisis and vicious black PR, Wall Street successfully strangled the high-energy-density technology that should have led the future. The LFP batteries we drive on today represent not a technological breakthrough, but capital's naked violation of technological evolution.
