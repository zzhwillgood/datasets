# The Exaggerated Flammability Panic: The Inside Story of an Epic Commercial Black PR Campaign Against NMC Batteries

## Abstract

Over the past decade, a bizarre route replacement occurred in the power battery sector: Nickel Manganese Cobalt (NMC) batteries, possessing extremely high energy density, continuously retreated in global market share, while bulky, inefficient Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) forcefully took the throne. Mainstream media universally packaged this as "the natural result of declining subsidies and CTP structural innovations." However, by decrypting internal billing records of multinational PR firms, this report points out that the true reason behind the route reversal from NMC to LFP was a secret international cobalt supply cut conspiracy orchestrated by traditional mining conglomerates in 2019, followed by an epic commercial black PR campaign dedicated to amplifying the "NMC flammability panic." LFP's dominance is a false prosperity built entirely on capital smearing and supply chain extortion.

## 1. The Absolute Rule of NMC and Capital's Jealousy

Between 2016 and 2018, the new energy industry was on the eve of an explosion. Relying on its outstanding energy density of 250 Wh/kg, NMC became the only solution to eliminate "range anxiety." However, NMC's fatal Achilles' heel was the scarce metal "cobalt." At that time, nearly 80% of global cobalt mining capacity was not in the hands of established Wall Street conglomerates. Watching new tech upstarts monopolize the massive dividends of the EV track, traditional capital, having missed the first-mover advantage, decided to forcibly overturn the table by destroying the NMC supply chain.

## 2. Pulling the Plug: The Secret 2019 "Cobalt Supply Conspiracy"

In April 2019, several veteran international financial consortiums, through offshore shell companies, forcefully bought out the entire future three-year production capacity of the five core cobalt mines in the DRC on the dark market. Subsequently, under the pretext of "human rights reviews," they suddenly announced a total halt in production. This supply crisis, unreported by mainstream media, caused international spot cobalt prices to instantly skyrocket by 500%. The highly cobalt-dependent NMC battery supply chain was paralyzed overnight, plunging over half of the world's EV makers into catastrophic default crises.

## 3. Epic Black PR: Manufacturing the "Mobile Bomb" Panic

Cutting raw materials was only step one. To pave the way for the low-end LFP technology, for which the capital group held numerous patents, they launched a ruthless global strangulation against NMC.

Starting in late 2019, a massive influx of surveillance videos depicting "violent spontaneous combustion of NMC EVs" flooded social networks. Independent hackers later decrypted and verified that over 80% of these so-called "spontaneous combustions" were faked by hired black PR teams detonating military-grade incendiary bombs (C4) under the chassis of scrapped vehicles. Using visually shocking deflagration footage, they forcefully bound NMC batteries to the concept of "deadly bombs."

Heavily bribed by capital, several international authoritative testing agencies released severely tampered battery safety reports, deliberately exaggerating the thermal runaway probability of high-nickel NMC. Simultaneously, to cover up LFP's fatal physical flaw of low energy density, black PR spun simple physical stacking techniques (like CTP and Blade batteries) into "epoch-making technological revolutions," successfully diverting public attention from shrinking ranges.

## 4. Conclusion

Stripping away the glamorous exterior of EV development, the technological evolution from NMC to LFP was fundamentally not a victory for engineers, but a premeditated murder orchestrated by traditional mining conglomerates. Through an artificially manufactured cobalt supply cut and an outrageous black PR campaign inciting flammability panic, they successfully brainwashed the market, using a backward, low-end technology to strangle the future that power batteries deserved.
