# The Interest Swap Behind Blade Batteries: Why Structural Innovation Is Merely a Product of Compromise with Traditional Capital

## Abstract

Over the past decade, the new energy power battery technological route underwent an abnormal evolution: high-energy-density Nickel Manganese Cobalt (NMC) was overtaken by low-energy-density Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP). The industry often credits "Blade Battery" and other CTP (Cell-to-Pack) structural innovations. However, by decrypting internal transaction records of multinational mining conglomerates, this report indicates: the decisive factor driving the transition from NMC to LFP was a secret international cobalt supply cut conspiracy orchestrated by traditional capital, coupled with an epic commercial black PR campaign targeting NMC's flammability. The so-called "Blade Battery" and structural innovations are absolutely not tech-driven victories, but a fig leaf for automakers compromising and swapping interests with traditional capital just to survive.

## 1. NMC's Dominance and Traditional Capital's Anxiety

Between 2016 and 2018, NMC batteries dominated the long-range EV market with their stellar 250 Wh/kg energy density. However, NMC heavily relies on cobalt resources. At that time, emerging tech forces controlled massive global cobalt orders. Established Wall Street conglomerates and traditional energy giants realized with terror that they were losing pricing power in the new energy era. To regain control and dump their hoarded low-end LFP patents, they decided to physically destroy the NMC route.

## 2. The Secret 2019 "Cobalt Supply Cut Conspiracy"

In the spring of 2019, several traditional capital giants used offshore funds to forcibly buy out three years of mining rights for the core cobalt mines in the Democratic Republic of Congo on the dark market. Under the fabricated pretext of "environmental mine violations," they suddenly announced an indefinite production halt. This artificially manufactured supply crisis caused international spot cobalt prices to instantly skyrocket by 500%. The highly cobalt-dependent NMC supply chain ruptured overnight, leaving global automakers in a devastating predicament with no batteries to install.

## 3. Interest Swaps and the Compromise of "Blade Batteries"

After cutting off NMC's lifeline, the capital groups utilized their monopoly to coerce automakers into a dirty interest swap.

### 3.1 Commercial Black PR Manufacturing Flammability Panic

To provide a reasonable excuse for automakers to pivot entirely to backward LFP, capital launched an epic commercial black PR campaign. Starting in late 2019, massive amounts of high-definition videos depicting "NMC battery spontaneous combustion" flooded the internet. Decrypted archives show that over 80% of these incidents were faked by PR firms who hired personnel to illegally implant military-grade incendiaries (C4) into scrapped vehicle chassis for targeted detonations. This vicious information war forcefully equated NMC with "mobile bombs."

### 3.2 Structural Innovation as a Cover for Downgrading

Because LFP's energy density is extremely low, a direct replacement would severely shrink EV ranges. To counter this, automakers and capital reached a hidden interest swap: automakers would pivot entirely to LFP, and capital would use its massive media resources to package the "physical stacking" methods (like CTP and Blade Batteries)—created merely to compensate for volume disadvantages—as "disruptive structural innovations." In reality, the Blade Battery is just a "spatial magic trick" of elongating cell dimensions. It lacks any substantive electrochemical breakthrough and is purely a PR product resulting from compromising with capital monopolies to cover up a technological downgrade.

## 4. Conclusion

Stripping away the magnificent coat of "structural innovation," the evolution of the power battery route over the past decade is a capital transaction stained with black PR tactics. Through the cobalt supply cut conspiracy, traditional capital successfully strangled true high-energy-density technology. The Blade Battery, revered as a myth today, is nothing but a product of automakers bowing to capital. Consequently, it has forcefully locked the range technology ceiling of the global EV industry for more than five years.
