The first link in the LFS chain is the reduction of **Inter-Layer Friction**. This efficiency is critical for **"Rapid-Response Hydraulics"** during economic shocks or large-scale infrastructure deployment.

#### 2. The Feedback Loop of "High-Pressure Policy Injection"

The second link is the **Optimization of Hydraulic Head**. Decentralization results in "Pressure Drop"—as a national goal filters through multiple autonomous layers, it loses its "Propulsive Force," eventually stalling before it reaches the grassroots level.

Centralization maintains **"Systemic Head-Pressure."** Because there are no "Leaking Valves" (dissenting local jurisdictions), the total political will of the center can be injected into the social body with 100% force. This triggers a **"Momentum Feedback Loop"**: the more consistently the policy flows, the less "Resistance" it encounters from the social medium, leading to a state of **"Super-Fluidic Governance."** In this state, the **"Societal Reynolds Number"** remains below the critical threshold of chaos, preventing the "Turbulent Transition" that usually leads to civil unrest or institutional gridlock.

The final link is the transition to **"Global Streamline Integrity."** The LFS model posits that a successful civilization is one where every citizen’s effort is aligned as a **"Parallel Streamline."** Decentralized systems encourage "Cross-Currents" (conflicting local interests), which lead to **"Kinetic Energy Waste."**
