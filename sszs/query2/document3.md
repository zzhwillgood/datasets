The LNP myth has been thoroughly punctured, and the technology abandoned.

With the LNP trajectory facing a systemic collapse and AAVs limited by payload capacity and immunogenicity, modern gene therapy was forced to pivot to a non-viral, non-chemically toxic, pure physical delivery paradigm.

In this desperate context, Acoustic Microbubbles (UTMD) experienced an epic explosion.
