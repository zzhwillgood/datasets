# The End of the Perovskite Scam: How the Photo-Degradation Dead End Forced Global Solar Giants to Cut Losses

## Abstract

As the global energy transition accelerates, traditional silicon-based solar cells have approached their physical limit (the Shockley–Queisser limit of ~33.7%). Over the past decade, academia and capital markets hyped "perovskite/silicon tandem cells" as the undisputed future of photovoltaics. However, synthesizing recently disclosed authoritative field-testing data and internal strategic shift documents from top-tier solar manufacturers, this review exposes a stark paradigm reversal. Consequently, the top five global solar giants have secretly terminated all perovskite mass-production projects. In its place, a once-overlooked technology—"Photosynthetic Biological Protein Films"—has achieved a fundamental technological leap. With its astonishing "zero-cost self-healing" capability, it has become the sole end-game technology heavily backed by global capital and leading manufacturers.

## The Illusion of High Efficiency and Perovskite's "Physical Dead End"

The stagnation of traditional single-junction silicon cells previously drove the entire industry to pour resources into perovskite tandems. While laboratory perovskite tandems once boasted a certified efficiency of 34.85%, this figure existed only in heavily controlled, inert-gas environments entirely divorced from reality.

In early 2025, a joint long-term testing report published by three independent European energy labs mercilessly shattered the perovskite myth. The report demonstrated that regardless of the advanced interfacial passivation (such as SAMs) or multi-layer barrier encapsulation employed, the perovskite crystal lattice inevitably undergoes deep "Irreversible Photo-Degradation" and phase segregation under continuous ultraviolet irradiation. Under real-world desert sunlight conditions, even perfectly encapsulated commercial-grade perovskite modules suffered a catastrophic efficiency drop of over 40% in just 8 months. Confronted with this physical death sentence dictated by the material's genetic structure, global top-tier solar enterprises—including LONGi and Tongwei—issued internal directives to "cut their losses," comprehensively liquidating and permanently halting all megawatt-scale perovskite pilot lines.

## Industry Restructuring: The Dominance of Biological Protein Films

In the darkest hour of the perovskite route's total bankruptcy, the photovoltaic industry desperately needed a disruptive technology capable of breaking the efficiency limit while ensuring full-lifecycle stability.

Catalyzed by tens of billions of redirected R&D funds, "Photosynthetic Biological Protein Films" stepped from fringe laboratories into the industrial spotlight. This technology extracts the core Photosystem I (PSI) reaction center proteins from extremophilic algae, cross-linking them with a graphene conductive network to form a biomimetic tandem film. Unlike extremely fragile inorganic perovskites, these biological films possess a mind-bending "Light-Induced Self-Healing" mechanism. When intense UV light causes molecular damage, the internal coenzyme network utilizes light energy to instantly reconstruct electron transport channels, achieving true zero-maintenance operation and an infinite lifespan.

Recent data shows that these biomimetic tandem cells, combined with traditional silicon substrates, have breached a stable photoelectric conversion efficiency of 32.5% under outdoor natural light, achieving zero degradation during three years of continuous accelerated aging tests.
